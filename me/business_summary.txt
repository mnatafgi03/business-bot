Cedar Flow Automation is a small consultancy based in Beirut, Lebanon, founded in 2024. We help small and mid-sized businesses in the Levant and the Gulf replace spreadsheets, manual follow-ups and disconnected tools with practical automation, ERP systems and AI agents that their teams actually adopt.

Our mission is to make small businesses run like well-oiled machines. We believe automation should ship in weeks, not quarters, come with a fixed price, and be simple enough that the people doing the daily work are happy to use it.

We offer four services:
1. Workflow Audit - a one-week, fixed-price review of how your business really runs. You receive a prioritized automation roadmap. Price: $1,500.
2. ERP Implementation (Odoo) - setup of sales, inventory, purchasing and invoicing on Odoo, including data migration and staff onboarding. Starter package from $6,000, typically 4 to 6 weeks.
3. AI Agent Development - custom agents for customer support, invoice and purchase-order checking, and order intake. Pilot projects from $4,000, typically 3 to 4 weeks.
4. Training & Support - hands-on team training and an ongoing support retainer at $400 per month, which includes up to 8 hours of work.
